Verify through the FastAPI ASGI route whether a medicine return with HOAN or DOI can refund a cycle that was paid, dispensed, then voided; verify the GIU_LAI path and the ledger recomputation. No production database was used.

src/tests/services/test_audit_1910_voided_return_http_probe.py
The probe source was temporary and was removed after the run. It is not a tracked test and is not preserved in this note. The test created an isolated paid medicine-cycle fixture for a 10-unit prescription, dispensed all 10 units, checked out, then voided the cycle. It exercised the general refund service as a control and posted to `/api/v1/pharmacy/khach-tra` through the ASGI app with FastAPI pool/identity overrides and an `Idempotency-Key`. HOAN returned 409; GIU_LAI returned 200. The probe then asserted the observed balance remained PHAI_THU=50,000 / PHAI_TRA=0 before and after GIU_LAI and that no `so_no` ledger row was written for `medicine.returned`.
